Urban Design & Planning Independent Urban Planning Project | X-Control, Afgoi, Somalia

X-Control Afgoi Urban Development Plan

An independent urban planning and development project prepared for X-Control, Afgoi. The project focuses on understanding the existing settlement, analysing its physical conditions, and developing a practical urban development concept.

The project combines site analysis, GIS mapping, land-use planning, road network planning, 2D master planning, and 3D urban visualization.

X-Control — Analysis 01

Existing Site & Urban Context

The existing settlement was studied using satellite imagery and spatial information to establish the wider physical context of the planning area.

Site context provided the starting point for the following building, land-use, road, and urban development analyses.

X-Control — Planning Concept

Urban Planning Objectives

The proposed development concept was organized around a number of practical planning objectives.

  • Analyse the existing settlement and physical conditions.
  • Develop a structured urban layout and road network.
  • Organize residential, commercial, and mixed-use areas.
  • Identify suitable locations for community facilities.
  • Introduce public parks, resting areas, and community spaces.
  • Improve connectivity and overall spatial organization.
